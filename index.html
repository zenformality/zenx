04 · about me

Just a student who builds things

I'm a student who still hasn't figured out what to do with my life — so I keep busy building random things: web apps, Python experiments, games, and the occasional AI tinkering. Most of it ends up on GitHub, whether it works or not.

I spend most of my time in Python, TypeScript, and JavaScript, with side quests in Go and Flutter/Dart. My projects tend to start with "what if…" and end with "…well, that was a learning experience."
