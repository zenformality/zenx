04 · about me

Just a student who builds things

I'm Prakit Chetia, though most people online know me as Zenx (or just Zen). I'm a student who still hasn't figured out what to do with my life, so I keep busy building random things: web apps, Python experiments, games, and the occasional AI tinkering. Most of it ends up on GitHub, whether it works or not.

I spend most of my time in Python, TypeScript, and JavaScript, with detours into Go and Flutter/Dart. My projects tend to start with "what if…" and end with "…huh, it actually ran."
